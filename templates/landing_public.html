Questions, answered

Know what happens before you connect anything.

Can I use my own AI provider?

Yes. Configure an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, API key, and model per workspace. Keys are encrypted and masked.

Does it send the same CV to every company?

No. Each campaign is researched and tailored independently. Generated results must validate before sending.

Can I review before sending?

Yes. Preview the exact email and generated RenderCV PDF before delivery.

Can I stop a campaign?

Pause the workspace or cancel a queued job. Suppression requests are honored immediately.
